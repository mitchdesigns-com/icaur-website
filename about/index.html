iCAUR Story

iCAUR is a new generation automotive brand redefining intelligent mobility through bold design, advanced technology, and expressive driving experiences. Built for the young and young-at-heart, iCAUR combines rugged character, smart innovation, and lifestyle-driven thinking to create vehicles that feel as exciting as they look.

Today we design and deliver vehicles that turn heads in Cairo traffic and conquer the dunes outside Alexandria. Every iCAUR is engineered for the heat, dust, and texture of Egypt — and built to last.

0 Engineers & Designers All based in Egypt, building for Egypt.
0 Years of R&D Prototyping and testing on real roads.
0 Groundbreaking Models V27 and O3T — two distinct personalities.
0 Made in Egypt Designed, engineered, and assembled locally.

Our Mission

We exist to put Egypt on the global EV map — engineering world-class electric vehicles at home, for the heat, the highways, and the people who drive them. Mobility, reimagined for real life.

Our Vision

To put Egypt on the global stage of electric mobility — engineering vehicles that don't just compete with the world's best, but define what's next for an entire generation.
